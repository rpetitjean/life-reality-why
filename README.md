# Life, Reality, Why

An existentialist exploration through video that confronts the fundamental questions of human existence and the vertigo of being.

## Overview

This project is a meditative journey into existential experience. Through visual narrative and reflection, it explores the uncertainties that shape consciousness, identity, and the search for meaning.

## Three Central Ideas

### The Vertigo of Life

The dizzying realization that existence offers endless possibilities without providing a guaranteed meaning. To be alive is to face freedom, uncertainty, responsibility, and the anxiety of choosing how to live.

### The Impossibility of Reality

Reality is never experienced completely or without interpretation. Perception, memory, language, and emotion shape everything we encounter, leaving us searching for what lies beyond our individual point of view.

### The Everlasting Question Why

Why do we exist? Why am I here? Why is there something rather than nothing? These questions may never receive final answers, but they continue to drive reflection, creativity, and the human search for meaning.

## About the Videos

The videos are free to be used for educational, personal, and contemplative purposes. They are offered as invitations to pause, reflect, and explore your own questions about life, reality, and existence.
